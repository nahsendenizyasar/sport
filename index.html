About me

A personal sports archive: running, triathlon, diving and table tennis as part of one continuous story.

Before the times and medals, there is continuity.

For me, sport is not only the medal on race day. It is a way to reset, rebuild discipline and start again when life gets heavy.

I was born in Eskişehir in 1978. In 2006, when I joined the 1st ÖGER Half Marathon, I did not really know what 21 kilometers would open up in my life. I finished that first half marathon in 2:12:59. After that, I began waiting for the next race, the next target and the next version of myself.

Over time, running became marathons, triathlons, open-water swimming and diving. In Germany I joined races, trained with teams, and later rediscovered competition through table tennis. This page is not meant to show a perfect athlete profile. It shows a real path: effort, setbacks, discipline and the decision to keep moving.

Diving

Diving is a different discipline from racing: instead of speeding up, you slow down, control yourself and create safety underwater.

CMAS 1★ Diving Instructor

Diving deserves its own place on this page. Running and triathlon are more visible from the outside; diving is quieter, but it has a strong character for me.

Underwater, rushing does not help. Breathing, calmness, attention and responsibility matter. The instructor side begins there: not just diving, but creating confidence for someone else.
